Quirsagax!

This page is about Quirsagax (Quir-sa-gax), a protagonist in my game design. He's a dragon that lurks within the city, hoarding knowledge, and an ally of Contrast. His name is a reflection of this, coming from the latin root words for search/seek and wise.
